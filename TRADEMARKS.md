# Trademark policy

The code of Resonator is open source under the Apache License 2.0. The
name is not. "Resonator", the Resonator logo and app icon, and the app
identifiers `am.resonator.*` are trademarks of Resonator LLC. The Apache
License says so itself (section 6): it grants no rights to use them.

This policy keeps one thing clear for everyone: an app or a service
called Resonator comes from Resonator LLC.

## What you may do without asking

- Use, build and run the code, including commercially.
- Run your own Resonator server under your own domain, and say that it
  runs Resonator ("powered by Resonator", "a Resonator server").
- Redistribute unmodified builds made from this repository, under the
  name Resonator, as long as you do not suggest that Resonator LLC made
  or endorses your distribution.
- Say truthfully that your product works with, connects to or is
  compatible with Resonator.
- Write about Resonator, review it, teach it and link to it.

## What needs a different name

If you change the code and distribute the result (a fork), or ship it
inside another product:

- Give it a name of your own that is not confusingly similar to
  "Resonator" (not "Resonator Plus", "ResonatorX", "Rezonator").
- Replace the logo and app icon.
- Use your own app identifiers (bundle ID, Android package name), your
  own app-store listing and your own signing keys.
- Do not point users at the official servers (`rsntr.net`,
  `resonator.network`, their relays) as if they were yours. Federating
  with them as a separate server is fine.

## App stores

Only Resonator LLC publishes apps named Resonator in the Apple App Store,
Google Play or any other app store. Builds of the official apps are
signed by Resonator LLC and published from this repository's release
pipeline.
